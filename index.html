Hello! Welcome to my portfolio! I am a computer programmer and game designer from Brantford, Ontario. I have graduated from the Wilfrid Laurier University Game Design and Development program and more recently graduated from the Computer Programming program at Conestoga College. So far I have helped develop and design numerous prototypes for a variety of different goals that can later be refined in to fully functioning applications. My portfolio consists of a mix of digital and analog projects. The portfolio here is a good representation of my skills and the direction I am heading.
