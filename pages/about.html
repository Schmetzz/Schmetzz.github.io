About Me

My name is Gabriel Hancock, and I am a game designer from Brantford, Ontario. I graduated from Wilfrid Laurier University with a bachelor of arts specializing in game design and development. I have created numerous prototypes and helped publish a table-top game called Suspect. I value creating games that focus on delivering unique experiences by taking in to account how the player interacts when creating content for them to explore, so that I can keep them engaged with an enjoyable experience.

Highlights of my skills:

  • Systems thinker: I view the whole project and understand what each component does. This includes analyzing what needs to be improved, cut, or maintained.
  • Project Management and Organization: I excel at organizing my time to meet deadlines.
  • Flexible Problem Solver: I can handle major changes and potential issues that arise from the iterative game design process.
